Before considering a policy

Questions worth asking.

  • Which care settings and services does this specific policy cover?
  • What must happen before benefits can be paid, and how does the waiting period work?
  • What are the benefit limits, exclusions, and application requirements?
  • Can premiums change, and how would ongoing costs fit my budget?
